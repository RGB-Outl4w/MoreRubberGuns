Two kinds of rubber

Light · Stinger

Stun, never wound

Every hit stuns the target like a beanbag and drains their will to fight. The damage is a token 0.1, so even a full magazine won't put anyone down.

MK18 · MP5 · G19 in Simunition blue

Heavy · Baton

Knock down, don't kill

Rubber batons hit hard enough to put a suspect on the ground, wounded but alive. As with the beanbag, head shots and point-blank hits can still kill.

LVAR · 1911 in less-lethal orange

The arsenal

Every gun keeps the original's model, animations and full attachment list (minus suppressors), with properly aligned scopes.

How it plays

Light guns

A couple of hits usually end with a suspect on their knees. Use them to make people comply, not to win firefights.

1911 LL Baton

The beanbag with more punch. Each hit stuns hard and costs a lot of morale. If they still won't comply, 3 body hits put them down (2 up close).

LVAR LL Baton

Built for sustained fire. Suspects shake the stun off in half a second and stay hostile, so you can't close a 10–20 m gap. Keep firing: about 5 body hits put them down.

Install in a minute

  1. Download

    Grab pakchunk99-Mods_MoreRubberGuns_P.pak from the latest release.

  2. Drop it in

    Copy the file into the game's Paks folder:

    …\steamapps\common\Ready Or Not\ReadyOrNot\Content\Paks\

  3. Gear up

    Start the game and open Loadout. The guns are in the Less Lethal tab of the primary and secondary slots.

Multiplayer: every player in the lobby needs the mod. To uninstall, delete the .pak.

Compatibility: built for Steam build 24942528 (Unreal Engine 5.3). Mods that replace AmmoDataTable, scope blueprints or the Russian engine locres will conflict. Details

Built from your own game files

The repository stores no game assets. A Python script extracts the vanilla weapons from your install, turns them into rubber guns and packs the mod. After a game patch, rebuild against the new files with a single command.
